Audit Platform
01 • The Problem

Why a Digital Audit Platform?

  • Paper checklists are slow, error-prone and hard to track
  • No real-time visibility of audit progress across teams
  • Evidence (photos, documents, audio) scattered across devices
  • Role confusion — who approves, who edits, who reports
  • Reports arrive late, inconsistent format, hard to audit later
  • No analytics to detect recurring non-conformances

Goal: one platform covering the full audit lifecycle — planning to sign-off.

Signup
05 • Secure Auditor Signup

Invite-Code Approval Flow

Three mandatory credentials

  • Pre-allocated Official Email ID
  • Password (strength meter + confirmation match)
  • Special Registration / Invite Code

Account state machine

1
Signup → status becomes PENDING_APPROVAL (holding screen, no dashboard access)
2
Admin / Manager / Supervisor see a Pending Approvals queue, verify email + code
3
Approve or Reject → on approval, immediate login + dashboard access

Invite codes are generated/allocated by approvers and bindable to a specific email.
